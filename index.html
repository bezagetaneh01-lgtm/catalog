What is Leak Data

Draft

Leak Data is the set of records generated and maintained across the full lifecycle of a gas leak — from initial discovery through classification, response, repair, and closure. It answers questions like: where and when was a leak found, how severe is it, what work has been done about it, and what is its current status.

In practice this covers four things: the leak itself (location, grade, status, cause — Section 4), the field readings and grading history that justify its classification, the work orders raised against it (emergency response, monitoring, surveillance, repair — each a distinct order type with its own detail form), and the historical audit trail of every status and grade change. Together these support both day-to-day field operations (dispatch, crew assignment, compliance tracking) and regulatory/backlog reporting (e.g. VW_LEAKS_BACKLOG).

2

What Application is Covered

Draft

The leak and work-order tables carry strong signals of an IBM Maximo-based work and asset management system as the operational source: work-order status codes such as WAPPR (waiting approval), WSCH (waiting to be scheduled), FLDCOMP (field complete), CLOSE, CAN (cancelled), and ASBUILT all match Maximo's standard status vocabulary, and MAXADMIN appears repeatedly as the system user recording status changes.

Field crews appear to capture leak-response and repair detail through structured digital forms (per work-order type — emergency odor investigation, monitoring, repair, repair observation, surveillance/wall check/recheck), which then land in Maximo as work order records before being extracted into this data mart. Columns like SF_ORDERTYPE and SF_WONUM suggest a Salesforce Field Service (or similar) layer may also be involved upstream of Maximo for field crew dispatch — this is inferred from the column prefix alone and should be confirmed.

Leak and work-order data is extracted from the operational system(s) into PROD_GDM_PROD_DB (Gas Distribution Management) on a scheduled refresh, tracked via SOURCE_REFRESH_DT / NGMOD_DT / NGLOADDATE columns throughout the Curated tables.

Leak grade (industry-standard convention)

Grade 1: hazardous, requires immediate action. Grade 2 / 2A: potentially hazardous, requires scheduled repair or periodic monitoring within a defined window. Grade 3: non-hazardous at time of detection, re-evaluated on a monitoring cycle. Exact repair/monitoring timelines are set by each jurisdiction's regulator and internal procedure, not shown in this data.

Work order status codes

Status values such as WAPPR, WSCH, FLDCOMP, CLOSE, CAN, ASBUILT, and change-by values like MAXADMIN indicate the leak/work-order tables are sourced from an IBM Maximo-based work-management system, feeding this data mart on a scheduled refresh (see SOURCE_REFRESH_DT / NGMOD_DT columns).
